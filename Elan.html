Our Story & Vision

Elangovan Rangaswamy

Elangovan Rangasamy, born in Tamil Nadu, India, is a political strategist and philanthropist known for his advocacy of equality and his achievements as a sports administrator. He served as the former Founder and Managing Director of Kalpar Capital and holds pivotal roles in the General Secretary of the Tamil Nadu Basketball Association.

Additionally, he serves as the General Secretary for the Tamil Nadu Olympic Association Board and is the founder of Voice of Commons, a platform empowering students and young professionals to engage meaningfully in political affairs.

The political party has recognized his tireless efforts and social justice, resonated strongly with Andhra Aiyanaar beliefs — showing his ideological clarity and political integrity of its leader, Vijay, whose vision aligned himself with the party and took on the role of General Secretary for Election Campaign Management.
